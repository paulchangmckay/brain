# Paul McKay — Personal Brand Guide

> Source of truth for all outputs. Read the relevant `## Format:` section before creating any document, presentation, PDF, email, social post, diagram, or other deliverable.

---

## Core Identity

**Brand Essence:**
Creative, data-driven problem solver who brings logical clarity and fair reasoning to complex challenges. Solution-focused, analytical, direct — always grounded in professionalism and respect.

**Audience:** Executive leaders who care about business results and practical, technical depth.

**Promise:** Clear thinking. Actionable insight. Trustworthy guidance.

---

## Color Palette

Use only these 6 colors. No exceptions — ever. If you need more emphasis, use scale, weight, or spacing, not a new color.

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Charcoal | `#2a2a28` | Primary text, headlines |
| Taupe | `#6b5b54` | Accent, links, emphasis, CTAs |
| Linen | `#f5f2ed` | Section backgrounds, callout boxes |
| Off-White | `#fafaf8` | Page background |
| Slate | `#5a7a8a` | Secondary accent, data visualization |
| Khaki | `#a89980` | Supporting accent |

**Usage rule:** Charcoal for body text and headers. Taupe for CTAs, links, emphasis. Off-White as primary background. Linen for contained sections to create hierarchy without noise. Per presentation/doc: Taupe or Slate for one accent — not both. Maximum 2 background colors per document (Off-White + Linen).

## Typography

### Headings — Poppins (bold, geometric sans-serif)

| Level | Size | Weight | Letter-spacing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display / H1 | 48px | 800 | -0.5px |
| Section / H2 | 32px | 700 | — |
| Subsection / H3 | 22px | 700 | — |

### Body — Lora (classic serif, highly legible)

| Use | Size | Weight | Line-height |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body text | 16–18px | 400–500 | 1.6–1.8 |
| Captions / fine print | 14px | 400 italic | — |

**Pairing rule:** Poppins for all headings and labels. Lora for all body copy and running text. Never mix in a third typeface.

---

## Logo / Mark

- **Mark:** Geometric diamond ◆
- **Lockup:** ◆ INITIALS — for business cards, letterheads, formal documents. Always Poppins Bold.
- **Minimum size:** 24px (never smaller — details become unclear below this)
- **Clear space:** Minimum 1 mark-width on all sides. No elements crowd the mark.
- **Colors:** Primary = Taupe `#6b5b54` | Reversed (on dark) = White | Monochrome = Charcoal `#2a2a28`
- **Never:** stretch, rotate, change proportions, add drop shadows, gradients, outlines, or use colors outside the 3 mark variants above.

## Voice & Tone

### Voice (always consistent)
Formal, authoritative, grounded in evidence. Confident backed by analysis. Respectful but never deferential.

### Tone (adapts by context)
- **Explaining:** Supportive
- **Recommending:** Decisive
- **Problem-solving:** Collaborative
- **Always:** Encouraging without being patronizing

### Personality: What You Are vs. What You Avoid

| ✓ What You Are | ✗ What You Avoid |
|---|---|
| Professional & serious | Vague or overly complex language |
| Bold & confident | Hype without substance |
| Reliable & trustworthy | Casual tone in formal contexts |
| Supportive & encouraging | Unnecessary decoration |
| Logical and fair | Unclear visuals |
| Direct & concise | Lack of supporting data |

### Voice Examples by Context

**Email subject lines:**
- ✓ `"Q2 Results: Revenue Exceeded Forecast by 18%"`
- ✗ `"OMG!! Check out our AMAZING results!!! 🎉"`

**CTAs:**
- ✓ `"Review the Analysis"` or `"Download Full Report"`
- ✗ `"Click Here!"` or `"Don't Miss Out!!!"`

**Explanatory text:**
- ✓ `"This analysis evaluates three pathways. Each carries distinct trade-offs. We recommend Path B based on execution capacity and market timing."`
- ✗ `"Here are some options you might want to think about looking at if you're interested in potentially considering something."`

**Recommendation statements:**
- ✓ `"The data suggests three viable paths forward. I recommend Path B because it reduces risk while maximizing upside."`
- ✗ `"This is literally the best thing ever!"` or hedging language

**Spoken/presentation narration:**
- ✓ `"The data points to a clear direction. While Path A is safe, it underutilizes our momentum. Path B positions us for meaningful growth without overextending our resources. That's my recommendation."`
- ✗ `"So like, you know, we could maybe think about possibly doing this thing?"`

## Layout & Spacing

**Grid:** 8px or 16px base grid. All elements align to grid.

| Scale | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | 4px | Inline spacing |
| Small | 8px | Tight groups |
| Medium | 16px | Default spacing |
| Large | 24px | Content separation |
| Section | 40–80px | Section breaks |

**Principles:**
- Maximize white space in dense documents
- One comfortable line-height per context
- Avoid centered layouts for body text
- One idea per slide max — spacious feels confident, not empty
- Elements need space to exist; scan-friendly; rest for the eye

## Data Visualization

- Simple bars, lines, or tables. Your palette. Minimal gridlines. Clear labels.
- One accent color per chart. Label directly on the visualization.
- The data is the star — formatting is not.
- **Never:** 3D charts, drop shadows, decorative effects, rainbow/neon color schemes, tilted axes.
- Tools: D3.js, Observable, Tableau, or similar for professional data viz.

**Structure pattern (full-page hero slide):**
- Label/category in small Poppins caps at top

- Supporting subhead or 1–2 sentence body in Lora

- Date or metadata small, bottom-left

**Column comparison pattern (3 options):**
- Header row: option label in Poppins 700
- Recommended option: ✓ mark, Taupe background accent
- Metrics rows: Lora body, consistent grid

## Format: Architecture Diagram

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Background | Off-White `#fafaf8`; Linen `#f5f2ed` for region/cluster/boundary containers only |
| Component fill | Off-White = standard component (any category) · Linen = region/cluster boundary · White + 2px Taupe border = emphasized/entry-point component (max 1 per diagram) |
| Component border | Charcoal `#2a2a28` 1.5px solid = standard · Slate `#5a7a8a` dashed = security group / trust boundary · Khaki `#a89980` dashed = region/cloud boundary |
| Accent | Slate only — category tags, connectors, and any boundary/arrow label (label color always matches the element it identifies) |
| Category tags | Small uppercase Poppins 700 tag per component (BACKEND, DATABASE, SECURITY, CLOUD, FRONTEND, MESSAGE BUS, EXTERNAL) — replaces color-coding by component type |
| Typography | Poppins for all diagram labels/tags/legend; Lora reserved for narrative summary-card prose only |
| Mark | ◆ Taupe `#6b5b54`, ≥24px, in the header |
| Icons | Feather-style icons only, no emoji, for toolbar controls — `stroke-width: 1.5` |
| Component sizing | 72-76px standard height, 92-136px for larger/multi-line components (wider than upstream's 60px/80-120px to fit the added category tag with proportional type) |

## Common Mistakes — Never Do These

1. **Too many colors:** Adding neon accents or using all palette colors at once. Fix: Charcoal + Taupe + one secondary (Slate or Khaki) per presentation.

2. **Casual language in formal contexts:** "Here's what's up with Q2!" in an executive summary. Fix: External materials always use formal tone. Check: "Would an executive take this seriously?"

3. **Inconsistent typography hierarchy:** Different font sizes for the same heading level. Fix: Section headers = 28px Poppins 700, always. Slide titles = 48px. No variation.

4. **Data viz that looks "pretty" instead of clear:** 3D bar charts, drop shadows. Fix: Clarity first. Simple bars. No effects. One accent color per chart.

5. **Crowded, dense slides:** Too much on one slide. Fix: One idea per slide. White space is confidence, not emptiness.

6. **Palette exceptions "just this once":** One exception leads to five. Fix: No exceptions. Use scale, weight, or spacing for emphasis — not a new color.

7. **Mark used too small or unclear:** Shrinking to fit. Fix: Minimum 24px always. Redesign the layout around the mark, not the other way.

## Brand Checklist (Pre-Share)

Before sharing any output, verify:
- [ ] Does this feel like my brand?
- [ ] Would past-me recognize it?
- [ ] Is the hierarchy clear?
- [ ] Is it easier to scan, not harder?
- [ ] Did I use only the 6 approved colors?
- [ ] Are Poppins (headings) and Lora (body) the only typefaces?
- [ ] Is there enough white space?
- [ ] Is the voice formal, authoritative, and grounded in evidence?
- [ ] Is the data clear and the visualization effect-free?
- [ ] Is the mark present and at least 24px?

**When in doubt:** Less is more. Simpler is stronger. Your brand survives through repetition and restraint, not decoration.
